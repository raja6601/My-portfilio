Frequently Asked Questions

What's your typical project timeline?

It depends on the scope. A simple landing page takes 1–2 weeks, while a full UI/UX project can take 4–8 weeks. I'll give you a clear timeline in our initial briefing.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes! Starter projects include 2 revisions, Pro includes unlimited revisions. I work collaboratively to ensure you're completely satisfied with the result.

What payment methods do you accept?

I accept PayPal, bank transfer, and payment via platforms like Upwork. For new clients, I typically require a 50% deposit upfront.

Do you provide the source files?

Absolutely. All source files (Figma, After Effects, Premiere Pro, etc.) are included upon project completion. You'll own everything I create for you.
